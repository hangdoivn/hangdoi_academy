# Media Career Program — Student Handbook Draft

> Internal draft for Cohort 01. Not candidate-facing until Legal & Enrollment gate is approved.
>
> Program length: 16 weeks / 4 months.
>
> Current system state: Register Interest only. Admission Send and Payment remain blocked.

## 1. Program purpose

Media Career Program is designed to develop a learner from entry-level potential toward **Production Ready** performance in commercial media work.

The program is not designed around attendance alone. Progress is demonstrated through evidence:

```
Learn
→ Practice
→ Feedback
→ Rework
→ Produce
→ Pass Standard
```

## 2. Learning tracks

All learners begin with a common foundation before role direction is finalized.

Potential directions:

- Photography
- Video / Camera
- Post-production
- Hybrid Media

Final direction may include:

- Primary Skill
- Secondary Skill
- Initial Production Role
- Starting competency level

## 3. Program outputs

A learner should finish with:

- curated Commercial Portfolio;
- Capstone Project;
- Competency Profile;
- Production Ready result;
- Primary / Secondary Skill direction;
- recommended initial role;
- clear development plan after completion.

Completion of the program does not automatically create an employment relationship.

## 4. Expected learner behaviour

Learners are expected to:

- arrive prepared and on time;
- follow production and safety instructions;
- protect client/internal data;
- handle equipment responsibly;
- communicate constraints early;
- accept feedback professionally;
- rework output when the standard is not met;
- collaborate without avoiding individual accountability;
- document file/data handoffs correctly;
- escalate risks instead of hiding mistakes.

## 5. Communication

Official operating channels should be defined before program launch.

Recommended structure:

- announcements: one official cohort channel;
- individual issues: Academy Coordinator;
- assignment submission: one controlled system;
- urgent production/safety issues: direct escalation to assigned mentor/coordinator.

Important instructions must not rely only on disappearing chat messages.

## 6. Attendance

Attendance is evidence of participation, not evidence of competency.

A learner can have strong attendance and still fail a competency standard.

A learner with insufficient attendance may lack enough evidence to be assessed.

Detailed rules are maintained in:

`media-career-attendance-assessment-policy-draft.md`

## 7. Assignments and deadlines

Each assignment must define:

- brief;
- output;
- deadline;
- technical constraints;
- assessment criteria;
- submission method;
- file naming;
- rework expectations.

Late work should be recorded, not handled informally.

Repeated late delivery is relevant to Production Behaviour assessment.

## 8. Feedback and rework

Feedback should identify:

1. what is not meeting the standard;
2. why it matters;
3. what change is required;
4. what evidence is needed for the next review.

Rework is a normal part of the program.

A learner should not be penalized merely for needing rework. The concern is repeated failure to learn from clear feedback or repeated process negligence.

## 9. Production exposure

Real-client exposure, if used, must have a defined educational purpose.

Learners must not be used as free replacement labour.

Before any real-client participation, the approved policy must define:

- scope of learner involvement;
- supervision;
- confidentiality;
- client approvals;
- IP/data restrictions;
- equipment responsibility;
- safety;
- working time/travel;
- remuneration where legally required;
- whether work may enter the learner portfolio.

## 10. Equipment

Equipment access is governed by a separate checkout/safety policy.

Core principles:

- no unauthorized borrowing;
- no unauthorized third-party use;
- no undocumented gear movement;
- no self-repair;
- immediate incident reporting;
- condition check at checkout and return.

See:

`media-career-equipment-safety-policy-draft.md`

## 11. Data and confidentiality

Learners may encounter:

- client briefs;
- unpublished campaign materials;
- footage/raw files;
- pricing;
- internal workflow;
- personal information;
- production plans.

Access is for program purposes only.

Do not:

- forward source files;
- upload client material to personal public storage;
- post BTS without approval;
- publish unfinished client work;
- share private briefs or rate cards.

See:

`media-career-ip-portfolio-confidentiality-draft.md`

## 12. Portfolio

Portfolio development is encouraged, but permission depends on asset type.

General categories:

- Academy simulation project: normally portfolio-eligible subject to policy;
- learner-original personal work: learner-controlled subject to third-party rights;
- real-client work: approval required;
- confidential/unreleased client work: not portfolio-eligible unless explicitly released.

## 13. Assessment

The program separates:

- attendance;
- assignment completion;
- competency evidence;
- production behaviour;
- final Production Ready decision.

No single score should erase a serious safety, integrity or data-handling concern.

## 14. Conduct and incidents

An incident should be documented when it involves:

- injury or safety risk;
- equipment damage/loss;
- client/data breach;
- serious conflict;
- repeated refusal to follow production instruction;
- harassment or threatening conduct;
- dishonest reporting;
- unauthorized publication of protected material.

The final disciplinary/termination process requires legal review before candidate-facing use.

## 15. Program completion

Completion should require all applicable conditions defined in the approved program document.

Recommended internal evidence set:

- attendance evidence;
- required module evidence;
- passed/reworked assignments;
- Capstone;
- competency profile;
- portfolio review;
- production behaviour record;
- unresolved incident status.

## 16. Academy → Production

The Academy and Production processes remain separate.

```
Program completion
→ Production Ready result
→ role/level review
→ separate Production recruitment decision
→ employment offer if applicable
```

No tuition payment purchases an employment position.
