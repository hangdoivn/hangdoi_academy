# Media Career Program — Attendance, Assessment, Rework & Retest Draft

> Internal operating draft. Final thresholds require alignment with the approved program and Enrollment Agreement.

## 1. Operating principle

The program measures **competency evidence**, not seat time alone.

Attendance answers:

> Was the learner present for required learning/production opportunities?

Assessment answers:

> Can the learner perform the required competency to the expected standard?

These must remain separate.

## 2. Attendance states

Recommended states:

- PRESENT
- LATE
- EXCUSED ABSENCE
- UNEXCUSED ABSENCE
- MAKE-UP COMPLETED
- MAKE-UP REQUIRED

## 3. Recommended minimum attendance

Working draft:

- overall required attendance: **85%+**
- mandatory practical/production labs: must be completed or formally made up
- mandatory safety/equipment induction: **100% before equipment access**
- Capstone-critical sessions: mandatory unless an approved make-up path exists

These numbers are operational proposals, not yet contractual terms.

## 4. Late arrival

Recommended internal rule:

- minor late arrival: log occurrence;
- material late arrival that misses briefing/safety instructions: learner may be held from the activity until re-briefed;
- repeated lateness contributes to Production Behaviour evidence.

Do not convert every late arrival into an automatic financial penalty.

## 5. Absence

Learner should notify the assigned coordinator as early as reasonably possible.

An absence may require:

- content catch-up;
- practice repetition;
- alternative evidence;
- rescheduled practical;
- inability to assess the competency if equivalent evidence cannot be generated.

## 6. Assessment evidence types

Use multiple evidence sources where appropriate:

- practical exercise;
- visual analysis;
- assignment;
- timed execution;
- production simulation;
- team exercise;
- file/data workflow;
- oral explanation;
- Capstone;
- mentor observation.

## 7. Competency result vocabulary

Recommended:

- NOT ASSESSED
- DEVELOPING
- MEETS STANDARD
- EXCEEDS STANDARD
- REWORK REQUIRED

Avoid vague labels such as:

- good;
- bad;
- talented;
- weak person.

Evidence should describe observable performance.

## 8. Rework

Rework should be required when output is recoverable and learning value remains.

A rework request should state:

- failed criterion;
- expected correction;
- evidence required;
- resubmission deadline;
- whether mentor review is available.

## 9. Retest

Retest is different from rework.

Use retest when the learner must demonstrate the competency again under a new or controlled task.

Recommended retest conditions:

- first assessment lacked enough evidence;
- learner completed required remediation;
- safety/critical workflow competence must be reconfirmed;
- original assessment environment was invalid.

## 10. Rework / retest limits

Do not set arbitrary limits only for administrative convenience.

The approved program should define where repeated failure means:

- extend/remediate;
- fail a module;
- become Not Production Ready;
- or terminate participation where applicable.

## 11. Critical competencies

Recommended critical areas where a high average score cannot compensate for failure:

- safety;
- data handling;
- equipment handling;
- serious confidentiality breach;
- dishonest evidence/reporting;
- repeated disregard of mandatory production instructions.

## 12. Production Behaviour evidence

Observe:

- punctuality;
- preparation;
- communication;
- role ownership;
- response to feedback;
- teamwork;
- equipment respect;
- data discipline;
- deadline reliability;
- escalation of errors/risks.

## 13. Weekly review

Recommended weekly learner review:

- strongest evidence this week;
- failed/rework evidence;
- process issue;
- behaviour issue;
- next-week priority;
- mentor action;
- learner action.

Keep weekly review short enough to operate consistently.

## 14. Mid-program review — Week 8

Review:

- attendance;
- common foundation;
- technical baseline;
- behaviour;
- Primary/Secondary Skill hypotheses;
- unresolved rework;
- risk of non-completion.

Output:

- Continue;
- Continue with Improvement Plan;
- Role direction requires more rotation;
- Serious risk review.

## 15. Final review — Week 16

Final review should assemble:

- competency evidence;
- Capstone result;
- portfolio;
- behaviour evidence;
- attendance completion;
- critical incidents;
- Primary/Secondary Skill;
- role recommendation.

Final outputs:

- PROGRAM COMPLETION result;
- PRODUCTION READY result;
- COMPETENCY PROFILE;
- ROLE DIRECTION.

Program Completion and Production Ready should not be assumed to be identical.
