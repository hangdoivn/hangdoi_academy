# Media Career Program — P2A Selection Runbook

## Objective

Selection is designed to identify candidates with the potential to become Production Ready, not candidates who already own equipment or already know the most software.

Selection evaluates:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Visual Potential | 25 |
| Learning Ability | 20 |
| Execution | 20 |
| Production Behaviour | 20 |
| Career Motivation | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

The total score supports the decision. It does **not** automatically create a PASS.

## Hard gates

A candidate should not PASS when there is a material concern around:

- safety
- data integrity
- serious dishonesty
- repeated failure to follow production instructions
- behaviour that creates material risk for clients/team
- inability to commit to the program schedule

These concerns override a high numeric score.

## 1. Visual Potential — 25

Assess how the candidate sees and interprets visual information.

Evidence may include:

- reference analysis
- image selection
- composition judgement
- observation of light, subject, hierarchy and emotion
- ability to explain why a visual choice works

Do not over-reward technical vocabulary.

A candidate may have strong visual potential without prior camera experience.

## 2. Learning Ability — 20

Assess how quickly the candidate can:

1. receive a new concept
2. understand the principle
3. reproduce it
4. adapt it to a slightly changed situation

Recommended exercise:

- mentor demonstrates one unfamiliar technique/workflow
- candidate repeats it
- candidate receives one correction
- candidate performs it again under a variation

Look for improvement between attempts.

## 3. Execution — 20

Assess whether the candidate can turn a small brief into an output under constraints.

Possible tasks:

- shoot a simple visual sequence
- create a 5–8 shot coverage plan
- select and sequence images
- complete a small edit
- execute a production task under a time box

Evaluate:

- task completion
- attention to brief
- prioritisation
- usable output
- time management

Do not grade only aesthetic polish.

## 4. Production Behaviour — 20

Assess how the candidate behaves inside a production-like environment.

Look for:

- listening
- asking useful questions
- following instructions
- respecting equipment
- teamwork
- communication
- responsibility for mistakes
- response to feedback
- data/safety awareness
- reliability

Recommended evidence:

a small team exercise where candidates must share roles and complete one output together.

## 5. Career Motivation — 15

Assessed primarily through interview.

Questions should clarify:

- Why Media?
- Why Commercial Production?
- Why now?
- What has the candidate already done to explore the career?
- How does the candidate respond to difficult feedback/rework?
- What would make them quit the program?
- What do they want to be doing in 3 years?
- Are they realistically able to commit for four months?

Avoid rewarding rehearsed motivational answers.

## Recommended selection flow

### Stage 01 — Application screening

Review:

- application completeness
- career intent
- visual references
- schedule compatibility
- tuition awareness
- obvious hard-gate concerns

Output:

- Qualified
- Lost
- Need Review

### Stage 02 — Selection session

Recommended group session:

**A. Visual Test — 30 min**

Reference selection / visual judgement.

**B. Learning Test — 40–45 min**

Teach → attempt → feedback → re-attempt.

**C. Execution Test — 45–60 min**

Time-boxed practical brief.

**D. Team Exercise — 30 min**

Production behaviour evidence.

Output:

provisional 85/100 from the first four dimensions.

### Stage 03 — Interview

Recommended:

20–25 minutes / shortlisted candidate.

Complete:

- Career Motivation /15
- behaviour clarification
- schedule/commitment clarification
- unresolved concerns

### Stage 04 — Decision review

CRM decisions:

- `PENDING`
- `PASS`
- `WAITLIST`
- `LOST`

Decision should consider:

- numeric score
- evidence quality
- hard gates
- available seats
- cohort balance
- program commitment

## Decision definitions

### PASS

Candidate has sufficient evidence to justify Admission review.

PASS is not Enrollment.

PASS is not Employment.

### WAITLIST

Candidate is suitable enough to retain but:

- seat availability is uncertain; or
- one material area needs comparison against the rest of the cohort.

### LOST

Candidate is not moving forward in the current cohort.

Lost Reason must be recorded in Candidate Operations.

### PENDING

Assessment is incomplete or decision has not yet been reviewed.

## Evidence discipline

Assessors should write observations, not personality labels.

Prefer:

> Reworked the lighting setup after one correction and improved exposure consistency on the second attempt.

Avoid:

> Smart candidate.

Prefer:

> Missed the backup instruction twice after confirmation and left the card unattended.

Avoid:

> Careless person.

## CRM workflow

For each candidate:

1. open Candidate Detail
2. complete five Selection scores
3. add Assessor
4. record Selection Notes
5. record Interview Notes
6. choose Decision
7. Save Assessment
8. assign Owner
9. set Next Action
10. record Lost Reason if applicable

Decision automatically syncs:

- PASS → pipeline `PASS`
- WAITLIST → pipeline `WAITLIST`
- LOST → pipeline `LOST`

All stage movements are logged in Stage History.

## Cohort target

Cohort 01 target:

**10 enrolled students**

Do not stop Selection at exactly 10 PASS candidates.

Maintain enough PASS/WAITLIST coverage for:

- candidate withdrawal
- schedule conflict
- financial constraint
- Admission decline

Final Enrollment belongs to P2B/P3 and occurs only after the applicable Admission and Enrollment process.
