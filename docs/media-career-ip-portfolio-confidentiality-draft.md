# Media Career Program — Confidentiality, IP, Data & Portfolio Draft

> Internal policy draft. Final IP/data rights require legal review and client-contract compatibility.

## 1. Asset categories

Every project should be classified before work begins.

### A. Learner Original

Created independently by learner without protected Academy/client source material.

### B. Academy Simulation

Training brief/assets created or licensed for the program.

### C. Academy Internal

Internal Hang Đôi material not approved for publication.

### D. Real Client

Material subject to client contract, approvals and confidentiality.

### E. Restricted / Confidential

Not permitted for personal storage/publication except as specifically authorized.

## 2. Default portfolio rule

| Asset type | Portfolio default |
|---|---|
| Learner Original | Allowed subject to third-party rights |
| Academy Simulation | Allowed after program review |
| Academy Internal | Approval required |
| Real Client | Written/recorded approval required |
| Restricted / Confidential | Not allowed |

## 3. BTS

No learner should assume “behind the scenes” means public.

BTS may reveal:

- unreleased products;
- talent;
- location;
- campaign concept;
- lighting/setup;
- client identity;
- commercial terms;
- source assets.

BTS publication requires the applicable approval.

## 4. Client material

Learners must not:

- upload source client files to public/personal-sharing links without approval;
- send client material to friends;
- use client footage for unrelated AI tools/services without permission;
- publish unreleased output;
- publish rejected work that reveals confidential material.

## 5. AI tools

Before uploading any client/internal asset to an AI service, verify:

- the asset is permitted;
- the service is approved;
- confidentiality/data terms are compatible;
- personal data is handled appropriately;
- client contract does not prohibit the use.

“AI workflow” is not an exception to confidentiality.

## 6. Storage

Approved storage locations should be defined before launch.

Recommended principle:

- source/client work stays in controlled Academy/Production storage;
- personal copies are not the default;
- access is least-necessary;
- completed projects follow an archive/retention process.

## 7. IP in training assignments

The approved Enrollment Agreement should clarify:

- learner ownership where applicable;
- Academy license to review/display training work;
- rights in collaborative work;
- rights in Academy-created briefs/assets;
- real-client rights controlled by client/production agreements;
- use for Academy marketing only where permitted.

## 8. Portfolio approval record

For each real-client item approved for portfolio, record:

- learner;
- project;
- exact asset/version;
- approval source;
- date;
- embargo date if any;
- attribution requirement;
- usage limitations.

## 9. Privacy

Candidate/student personal data and media-release permissions are separate issues.

Do not assume:

- participation in the course = consent to public marketing;
- appearing in BTS = consent to advertising;
- application consent = unlimited future marketing consent.

## 10. Breach response

If protected data/assets are shared incorrectly:

1. stop further sharing;
2. remove/revoke access where possible;
3. notify responsible coordinator;
4. preserve facts/evidence;
5. assess client/personal-data impact;
6. follow the approved incident process.

Do not hide or silently delete evidence.

## 11. Completion portfolio

Portfolio curation should prioritize:

- role clarity;
- strongest evidence;
- commercial relevance;
- honest contribution attribution;
- approved assets only.

Quantity is not the goal.

## 12. Candidate-facing finalization

Before publication, this draft must align with:

- client contracts;
- Enrollment Agreement;
- privacy terms;
- media release;
- Academy brand-use permissions;
- applicable IP/data law.
