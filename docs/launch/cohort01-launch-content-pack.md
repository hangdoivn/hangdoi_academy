# Asset 01 — Career Gap

Master Content ID:

`reel01-career-gap`

Recommended format:

- Reel / TikTok
- 25–35 sec
- face + production B-roll
- fast editorial pacing

## Hook

> Biết quay. Biết edit. Nhưng vì sao vẫn khó đi làm Media?

## Core idea

Most beginners learn tools in isolation.

Commercial production requires a system:

- understand a brief;
- prepare;
- shoot usable coverage;
- work with people;
- protect data;
- respond to feedback;
- deliver on time.

## Suggested structure

### 0–3s

On-screen:

**BIẾT QUAY ≠ ĐI LÀM ĐƯỢC**

Visual:

quick cuts of camera / timeline / lighting.

### 3–12s

On-screen sequence:

- Camera
- Premiere
- Lightroom
- Gimbal

Voice/content:

These are tools, not the entire job.

### 12–25s

Show:

- brief
- moodboard
- shot list
- lighting setup
- shooting
- ingest
- review
- rework
- final output

On-screen:

**Commercial Production = Workflow + Standard + Team**

### 25–35s

On-screen:

**4 months → Production Ready**

CTA:

**Cohort 01 đang mở Register Interest.**

## Caption angle

Learning Media becomes much faster when the learner understands what the job actually requires.
