## 2. Nature of participation

The activity is intended primarily for supervised learning.

Define clearly what the learner may and may not do.

Example permitted scope:

- observe workflow;
- assist under supervision;
- practice setup;
- capture training material where permitted;
- perform a defined learning task.

Do not assign unrestricted client responsibility merely because the learner is present.

## 3. Productive work / remuneration review

Before the activity, the operator must determine whether the learner will directly:

- perform labour contributing to the commercial deliverable; or
- create a product/output used commercially.

If so, counsel/HR/finance should confirm whether remuneration or a separate lawful arrangement is required.

[REMUNERATION / ARRANGEMENT / BASIS]

## 4. No employment implication

Participation in one production-learning activity does not itself guarantee or establish future employment.

Where an employment relationship is actually created, it must be documented separately under applicable labour law.
