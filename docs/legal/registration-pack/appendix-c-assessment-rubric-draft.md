# Appendix C — Assessment Rubric Draft

**DRAFT — PROGRAM / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**

This rubric is designed for program-completion evidence, not employee performance appraisal.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | No usable evidence / unsafe / unable to complete |
| 1 | Performs only with close step-by-step support |
| 2 | Performs a standard task with support/review |
| 3 | Performs the assessed entry-level task independently and reliably |
| 4 | Exceeds the assessed entry-level requirement and adapts to variation |

A final Production Ready result should not be determined by numeric average alone.

## C1 — Technical Foundation

Assess:

- camera/lens control;
- focus/exposure;
- lighting;
- audio/equipment where applicable;
- equipment safety.

## C2 — Visual & Commercial Judgement

Assess:

- brief interpretation;
- framing/visual hierarchy;
- shot relevance;
- commercial usability;
- visual consistency.

## C3 — Pre-production

Assess:

- research;
- references;
- moodboard;
- shot list;
- production preparation;
- resource awareness.

## C4 — Production Execution

Assess:

- assigned shot/task completion;
- technical consistency;
- production pace;
- coverage;
- adaptation under realistic constraints.

## C5 — Post-production & Workflow

Assess:

- ingest/select;
- edit/retouch;
- sound/colour where applicable;
- versioning;
- QC;
- delivery/archive.

## C6 — Production Behaviour & Communication

Assess:

- reliability;
- listening;
- instruction-following;
- teamwork;
- feedback/rework;
- professional conduct.

## C7 — Problem Solving & Reliability

Assess:

- diagnosis of common issues;
- appropriate escalation;
- practical solution;
- continuity under change.

## C8 — Ownership & Business Awareness

Assess at entry level:

- understanding of deliverable purpose;
- quality/deadline awareness;
- ability to recognize what makes output usable;
- responsibility for assigned task.

## Hard gates

### Safety

Automatic fail/review if there is unresolved material unsafe behaviour.

### Data Integrity

Automatic fail/review if the learner cannot demonstrate required media/file/backup handling.

### Serious Conduct

Automatic fail/review for unresolved conduct incompatible with production participation.

1. assessor records failed standard;
2. learner receives defined feedback;
3. revised/new evidence is submitted;
4. assessor records retest result;
