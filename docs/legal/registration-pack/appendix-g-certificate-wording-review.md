# Appendix G — Certificate / Completion Wording Review

**DRAFT — DO NOT ISSUE A CERTIFICATE FROM THIS FILE**

The exact completion document depends on the confirmed legal classification and registration requirements.

## Current rule

Do not publicly promise:

- diploma;
- degree;
- nationally recognized qualification;
- professional license;
- employment certification;

unless the exact legal basis is confirmed.

## Working options for counsel/authority review

### Option A — Training Certificate

Possible concept:

> Certificate of Completion — Hang Đôi Media Career Program

Use only if the operating entity has legal authority to issue the applicable certificate for the confirmed program classification.

### Option B — Internal Completion Record

Possible concept:

> Program Completion Record / Competency Profile

This may be operationally distinct from a statutory training certificate.

Legal review required on public wording and whether it can be issued alongside/instead of another required certificate.

### Option C — Production Ready Result

Possible concept:

> Production Ready Assessment Result

This should be described as an internal competency result, not a government-recognized qualification.

## Required fields to confirm

- legal title of certificate/document;
- issuing entity;
- authorized signatory;
- serial/numbering requirement;
- database/reporting requirement;
- date;
- program title/classification;
- learner identity fields;
- completion basis;
- security/recordkeeping;
- replacement/reissue process.

## Prohibited implication

No completion document should state or imply:

- guaranteed employment at Hang Đôi Production;
- guaranteed salary;
- automatic P-level/role;
- statutory qualification not actually awarded.
