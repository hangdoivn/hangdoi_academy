## 8. Equipment

Where Hang Đôi provides equipment, the learner must:

- follow equipment SOPs;
- use equipment only for authorized purposes;
- report loss/damage immediately;
- return equipment in the required condition.

Any learner liability for loss/damage must be determined fairly based on actual circumstances and applicable law; no blanket automatic charge should be applied without review.

## 9. Safety

The learner must comply with safety instructions for:

- electrical equipment;
- lighting/grip;
- travel/location work;
- night work where applicable;
- food/hospitality locations;
- client premises;
- lifting/moving production equipment.

The operator may stop participation where there is a material safety risk.

## 10. Data and file integrity

The learner must follow approved rules for:

- memory cards/media;
- backup;
- file naming;
- client data;
- confidential briefs;
- account credentials;
- delivery/archive.

Intentional or serious negligent handling of client/project data may result in removal from a production-learning activity and may trigger program conduct review.

## 11. Real-client production exposure

Real-client production exposure, if used, must follow the approved Production Exposure Terms.

Participation does not convert the learner into an employee unless a separate lawful employment arrangement is created.

Where applicable law requires remuneration for direct productive work or created output, the applicable arrangement must address that separately.

## 12. Intellectual property

### Training exercises

Unless otherwise stated in an approved exercise brief, learner-created training work should remain attributable to the learner, subject to any third-party assets, releases and confidentiality restrictions.

### Client/project work

Rights relating to real client work must follow the specific project brief, client agreement, release/consent and applicable law.

The Enrollment Agreement should not operate as a blanket assignment of all future learner-created intellectual property.

## 13. Portfolio use

Portfolio use of client/project material requires:

- client/project permission where applicable;
- release of embargo/confidentiality restrictions;
- compliance with privacy/image rights;
- correct credit where required.

The learner may not assume all produced material is portfolio-safe.

## 14. Image / likeness

Any use of the learner's identifiable image/voice for marketing should be governed by a separate or clearly separable consent mechanism where required.

Refusal of optional marketing consent should not by itself affect academic/selection treatment.
