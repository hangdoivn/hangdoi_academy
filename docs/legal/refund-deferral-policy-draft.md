## 2. Principles

The final policy should be:

- disclosed before payment;
- clear enough for a learner to understand total financial exposure;
- consistent with the Enrollment Agreement;
- applied consistently;
- documented in CRM/accounting;
- compliant with applicable consumer, education, tax and contract law.

## 3. Academy cancellation

If Hang Đôi cancels the cohort before it begins, the learner should be offered:

- full refund of amounts paid; or
- voluntary transfer/deferral to another approved cohort.

No forced credit-only outcome should be used without legal approval.

Target refund processing time: [X BUSINESS DAYS].

## 4. Academy postponement / material schedule change

Define a material postponement threshold:

[NUMBER OF DAYS / MATERIAL CHANGE TEST]

Learner options should include:

- accept revised schedule;
- approved deferral;
- refund according to the approved policy.

## 5. Learner withdrawal before start

Final policy decision required.

Counsel should approve a clear table for:

- withdrawal more than [X] days before start;
- withdrawal [X–Y] days before start;
- withdrawal less than [X] days before start.

Any retained amount must have a lawful/defensible basis and be clearly disclosed.

## 6. Learner withdrawal after start

Recommended structure for counsel review:

- distinguish services already delivered from future undelivered training;
- distinguish non-recoverable third-party costs only where valid and documented;
- define treatment of the current installment;
- define treatment of unpaid future installments;
- prohibit arbitrary penalty amounts not approved in the policy.

Final formula:

[APPROVED REFUND FORMULA]

## 7. Installment payment

Proposed plan:

40,000,000 VND total  
Possible schedule: 10,000,000 VND × 4 installments

Final agreement must define:

- installment due dates;
- grace period;
- reminder process;
- late/non-payment consequence;
- whether access pauses;
- whether future installments remain due after approved withdrawal.

No debt/penalty wording should be used without legal review.

## 8. Medical / emergency deferral

Possible qualifying evidence:

[TO DEFINE]

Possible options:

- one-time deferral;
- cohort transfer;
- partial refund;
- pause/re-entry.

The final rule should avoid intrusive collection of medical data beyond what is necessary.

## 9. Deferral

Define:

- maximum number of deferrals;
- valid period;
- seat availability;
- tuition-price difference treatment;
- curriculum-version changes;
- expiry;
- transferability (normally non-transferable unless approved).

## 10. Removal for conduct / safety

If a learner is removed for serious conduct/safety reasons, financial treatment must follow a pre-disclosed rule and applicable law.

Do not state "no refund under any circumstances" without counsel review.

Required process:

- incident record;
- applicable rule;
- learner notice;
- review/appeal where appropriate;
- financial calculation record.

## 11. Scholarship / tuition support

If a scholarship is offered, define:

- scholarship amount;
- whether it reduces tuition or is separately funded;
- effect of withdrawal/deferral;
- whether vested support can be clawed back;
- tax/accounting treatment.

Avoid structures that operate as employment security/deposit.
